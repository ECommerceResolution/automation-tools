About AI Automation

Our Story

AI Automation was founded with a clear mission: to help e-commerce store owners build professional, revenue-focused email automation systems — without the high cost and slow turnaround of traditional agencies.

We believe every store, whether it's just starting out or already generating millions, deserves access to well-built automations that recover lost revenue, improve customer experience, and support long-term growth.

Our team combines experience in e-commerce operations, customer lifecycle marketing, and automation systems. Every automation package we offer is built around real buyer behavior — not guesswork.
