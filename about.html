Our Story

AI Automation was founded with one mission: to help e-commerce store owners multiply their revenue through intelligent email automation. We believe every store — whether you're just starting or doing millions — deserves access to AI tools that were once only available to Fortune 500 companies.

Our team combines decades of experience in AI, email marketing, and e-commerce. We've helped over 2,800 stores across 47 countries increase their revenue with our tools.

We don't just sell software. We partner with you to grow your business. When your store makes more money, we succeed.
